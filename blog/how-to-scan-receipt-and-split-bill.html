How to Scan a Restaurant Receipt and Split the Bill

📅 10 September 2026 ⏱️ 4 min read ✍️ SplitEven Team
Quick Answer

Open SplitEven, photograph the receipt, add names, tap to assign each item to whoever ordered it, and share the breakdown as a link. The AI reads the receipt automatically — no typing. Most bills are done in under 60 seconds.

The old way to split a restaurant bill involved one person doing mental arithmetic while everyone else sat watching. Or worse, someone pulling out a calculator while the table went quiet and the mood shifted.

A receipt scanning bill splitter replaces all of that. The AI reads the receipt — every item, price, quantity, tax, and tip — the moment you point your camera at it. You tap to assign items to people. The app calculates everyone's exact share. You share a link. Done, before anyone has had time to reach for their wallet.

Here's the full process, step by step.

Step-by-Step: How to Scan a Receipt and Split the Bill

Step 1: Open the app and scan the receipt

Open SplitEven on your phone. Tap the scan button and point your camera at the restaurant receipt. Hold it steady for a moment — the AI captures the image and begins reading.

The AI extracts every line item, its price, any listed quantity, the subtotal, tax, and tip. It also detects the currency from the symbol on the receipt automatically, so if you're abroad, no manual currency selection is needed.

If the receipt is crumpled, the lighting is dim, or the print is faint, flatten it out and try again. Most receipts scan cleanly on the first attempt. If any item is misread, you can correct it manually before proceeding.

Step 2: Add the people splitting the bill

Enter the names of everyone splitting the bill — or pick from your contacts. You don't need phone numbers or email addresses. Just names, so you can assign items to specific people.

Step 3: Assign items to whoever ordered them

This is the core step. Each item from the receipt appears on screen. Tap an item, then tap the name of the person who ordered it. Their name appears on the item, and it's added to their running total.

For items ordered by the same person, this goes quickly. You get a rhythm: tap item, tap name, next item.

Step 4: Handle shared items

Two types of shared items need slightly different handling:

Equally shared items (a shared starter, a bread basket, a side dish everyone ate from): tap the item and assign it to multiple people. The cost divides equally among everyone you select. If four people shared a starter, each person is charged one quarter of the cost.

Items with different quantities (a bottle of wine where people had different numbers of glasses, a shared platter where consumption wasn't equal): tap the item, assign it to everyone involved, then tap Split Quantity. Set how many portions or glasses each person had. The cost divides proportionally — someone who had two glasses pays twice as much as someone who had one. For a detailed look at how this works with shared bottles and unequal orders, see our guide on splitting bills when everyone orders different amounts.

Step 5: Review the totals

Once all items are assigned, SplitEven shows each person's total: their items, their proportional share of tax, and their proportional share of any tip or service charge. Review to make sure everything looks right — especially that every item has been assigned and nothing has been missed.

Step 6: Share the breakdown

Tap Share. SplitEven generates a link to a full itemised breakdown. Send it to the group via WhatsApp, iMessage, or any messaging app.

Your friends open the link in their browser — no app download, no sign-up. They see their name, every item attributed to them, their share of tax and tip, and their total. They can verify it against what they remember ordering.

For a guide to sharing via WhatsApp specifically, see our article on how to split a bill on WhatsApp.

Tips for Faster, More Accurate Scanning

Flatten the receipt. A crumpled or folded receipt is the most common reason for scanning errors. Lay it flat on the table before photographing.

Good lighting helps. If the restaurant is dim, use your phone's torch or photograph the receipt near a light source. The AI can handle most print quality, but very faded thermal receipts in low light are harder to read.

Scan before you pay. Some restaurants print a separate itemised receipt and a payment slip. Scan the itemised one — it has all the line items. The payment slip often just shows the total.

You can edit any misread item. If the AI misreads a price or item name, tap it to correct it. This is rare but takes only a few seconds when it happens.

What Happens to Tax and Tip?

Tax and tip are handled proportionally. If your food came to 35% of the pre-tax subtotal, you pay 35% of the tax and 35% of the tip. People who ordered more pay more of both; people who ordered less pay less. This is the fairest way to handle it — fairer than splitting tax and tip equally regardless of who ordered what.
